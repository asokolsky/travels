# Public Transportation in Berlin

Part of the [GTC Berlin 2026](index.html) plan.

Berlin's [S-Bahn](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Berlin_S-Bahn),
[U-Bahn](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Berlin_U-Bahn),
[tram](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Trams_in_Berlin),
[bus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bus_transport_in_Berlin), and most regional
trains share one fare system run by
[BVG](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Berliner_Verkehrsbetriebe) and
[VBB](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Verkehrsverbund_Berlin-Brandenburg). Both hotels and both GTC venues are in fare zone AB;
only the BER airport trip needs the ABC extension. [[1]](#source-1)

- **Modes:** U-Bahn (U1, U2, U3, U7) and S-Bahn (S1, S2, S25, S26) cover the
  hotel-to-venue trips listed in the
  [conference commute](index.html#conference-commute); trams and buses (M29,
  M41) fill the gaps. Most of this trip is walkable, so transit is mainly for
  the airport and longer evenings.
- **Tickets:** As of October 9, 2026, BVG lists these standard fares starting
  at: single ticket 4.00 EUR (valid 2 hours, one direction), short trip
  2.80 EUR (3 S-/U-Bahn stops or 6 tram/bus stops), 4-trip ticket 12.40 EUR,
  and 24-hour ticket 11.20 EUR (valid 24 hours from validation).
  [[2]](#source-2)
- **Which to buy:** A single or 4-trip ticket suits a walking-heavy week. A
  24-hour ticket only pays off with four or more rides in a day.
- **Airport:** BER is outside zone AB, so buy an ABC ticket for the airport
  ride. The ABC single fare is higher than AB, and the BVG overview does not
  break it out by zone. TODO: confirm the ABC single and 24-hour prices in the
  BVG app or at a station machine before the trip. [[1]](#source-1)
  [[2]](#source-2)
- **Where to buy:** BVG or VBB app, station machines, or on board trams and
  buses. Digital tickets are active once purchased in the app; paper tickets
  must be validated before boarding.
- **Tourist pass:** The Berlin WelcomeCard (48 hours 28.50 EUR, 72 hours
  39.50 EUR, 4 days 49.50 EUR, 5 days 53.50 EUR) bundles transit with
  attraction discounts. It only makes sense with several paid sights; the
  [One-Day Berlin Urban Hike](one-day-hike.html) is mostly on foot.
  [[2]](#source-2)
- **Checks:** Ticket inspectors are common and there are no gates. Keep the
  ticket or app screen ready, and do not ride without a valid ticket.
- **Service changes:** Check BVG service notices for U-Bahn and S-Bahn closures
  and replacement buses before each commute.
