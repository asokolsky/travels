# GTC Berlin 2026

Assumptions: attend the conference and any booked workshop in Berlin, keep
flight and hotel details in TripIt, use public transportation between BER and
central Berlin, and do not rent a car.

**Plan to walk between the GTC venues.** STATION-Berlin and Tempodrom are in
the same neighborhood, separated by only a short walk. NVIDIA's venue page
specifically presents walking as the transfer between them. [[4]](#source-4)

[Walking route: STATION-Berlin to Tempodrom](https://www.google.com/maps/dir/?api=1&origin=STATION-Berlin%2C%20Luckenwalder%20Strasse%204-6%2C%2010963%20Berlin%2C%20Germany&destination=Tempodrom%2C%20Moeckernstrasse%2010%2C%2010963%20Berlin%2C%20Germany&travelmode=walking)

Useful public-transport stops:

- **Gleisdreieck:** U1, U2, and U3; about a 5-7 minute walk to
  STATION-Berlin.
- **Moeckernbruecke:** U1, U3, and U7; about an 8-10 minute walk to
  STATION-Berlin.
- **Anhalter Bahnhof:** S1, S2, S25, and S26; about a 3-5 minute walk to
  either conference venue.
- **Anhalter Bahnhof bus stop:** M29 and M41; about a 3-5 minute walk to
  either venue. [[4]](#source-4)

Do not plan to drive. Tempodrom has no public garage, street parking is scarce,
and STATION-Berlin has limited paid parking. NVIDIA recommends public transit
or nearby garages instead. [[4]](#source-4)

- **REWE Yorckstrasse** -- about a 20-minute walk; the useful fallback for a

  ([map](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=REWE%2C%20Yorckstrasse%2078%2C%2010965%20Berlin%2C%20Germany)).

Buy routine supplies on Monday or during the conference week rather than
assuming a neighborhood supermarket will open on Sunday.

### Restaurants

- **BRLO Brwhouse** -- about a 10-minute walk in Park am Gleisdreieck; the
  best nearby choice for a relaxed group dinner, Berlin-brewed beer, and
  seasonal food. Schoeneberger Strasse 16
  ([map](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=BRLO%20Brwhouse%2C%20Schoeneberger%20Strasse%2016%2C%2010963%20Berlin%2C%20Germany)).
  It is closed Monday, so use it after the first conference day rather than
  for a pre-conference dinner. [[10]](#source-10)
- **TorEins** -- about a 10-minute walk, on the German Museum of Technology
  grounds; a practical breakfast, lunch, coffee, or cake stop with German
  cooking and regional ingredients. Moeckernstrasse 26
  ([map](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=TorEins%2C%20Moeckernstrasse%2026%2C%2010963%20Berlin%2C%20Germany)).
  [[11]](#source-11)
- **Curry 36 Mehringdamm** -- about a 20-minute walk or a short U-Bahn trip;
  the fast, inexpensive Berlin choice for currywurst, fries, or a vegan
  sausage after a long conference day. Mehringdamm 36
  ([map](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Curry%2036%2C%20Mehringdamm%2036%2C%2010961%20Berlin%2C%20Germany)).
  [[12]](#source-12)

Conference-week strategy: reserve BRLO for a group, use TorEins when the
schedule allows a daytime break, and keep Curry 36 as the low-friction late
meal. Public Rick Steves restaurant guidance is thin for this immediate
neighborhood, so these picks emphasize official location information,
practical walking distance, and distinctly Berlin options.

## Airport: BER

**Best practical choice:** use the train between
[Berlin Brandenburg Airport](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Berlin_Brandenburg_Airport)
and central Berlin. STATION-Berlin's official directions recommend the FEX to
Potsdamer Platz, followed by the U2 to Gleisdreieck, and estimate about 40
minutes. Check the live BVG or VBB route on the travel day before committing to
that interchange. [[5]](#source-5)

[Transit route: BER to STATION-Berlin](https://www.google.com/maps/dir/?api=1&origin=Berlin%20Brandenburg%20Airport%20%28BER%29&destination=STATION-Berlin%2C%20Luckenwalder%20Strasse%204-6%2C%2010963%20Berlin%2C%20Germany&travelmode=transit)

The BER railway station is below Terminal 1 on level U2. FEX, regional, and
S-Bahn trains run between the airport and Berlin several times per hour. BER
is in fare zone C, so travel between the airport and central Berlin requires a
Berlin ABC ticket; buy it from a machine or in the VBB, BVG, or S-Bahn app.
[[6]](#source-6)

Use a taxi only for a very late arrival, heavy luggage, or a hotel that makes
the rail connection awkward. A car adds little value for a conference centered
on two walkable venues with good transit access.

## Practical Notes

- Install the BVG or VBB app before arrival. One ticket covers regional rail,
  S-Bahn, U-Bahn, tram, and bus within its fare zones. [[6]](#source-6)
- Compare single, four-trip, and 24-hour tickets once the hotel commute is
  known; BVG offers all three rather than requiring a tourist pass.
  [[7]](#source-7)
- Carry a light waterproof layer. Protect the short walks between transit,
  STATION-Berlin, and Tempodrom rather than planning taxi transfers.
- Leave the first evening light. Use it to confirm the hotel-to-venue route,
  collect groceries, and verify the opening session in the live schedule.
- Recheck the conference schedule, keynote time, BVG service notices, and BER
  rail departures during the week before departure.
